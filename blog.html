Kanchipuram vs Semi-Silk Saree

Pure Kanchipuram silk is heavy (800g–1.2kg), lustrous and expensive — ideal for grand weddings, but high-maintenance (dry clean only).

Semi-silk gives you that same rich look and zari shine at almost half the price, weighs ~800g, drapes easily, and — importantly — can be normal washed at home.

Our honest advice: for everyday wear, office and most festivals, semi-silk is the smarter buy. For the bride's main wedding saree, pure silk is the classic choice.

How to Drape a Semi-Silk Saree

1️⃣ Office style — pin the pallu diagonally, keep pleats straight, add a simple border blouse.
2️⃣ Party style — fan pleats + a stone pin on the shoulder.
3️⃣ Daily style — let the pallu hang free, low-maintenance drape.
4️⃣ Traditional — full pallu over the head for pujas.
5️⃣ Modern — belted drape over a shirt.

Semi-silk holds pleats well and doesn't slip like georgette — perfect for long events.
